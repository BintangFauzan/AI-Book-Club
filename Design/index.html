The beauty of the first try

When we begin something new, we bring with us the desire to get it right. But the first attempt is not a verdict. It is an opening.

We can give ourselves permission to make a mess. To follow a thread without knowing where it leads. To let curiosity move faster than judgment.

In this space, the work is not a performance. It is a conversation with what wants to come through.
